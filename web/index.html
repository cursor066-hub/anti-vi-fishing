What happens next

  1. Attach signed, action-bound evidence from eligible independent sources.
  2. Evaluate the exact capsule against the active constitution.
  3. Obtain independent signed approvals of the action, policy and evidence digests.
  4. Mint the single-use certificate only after ALLOW.
  5. Dry-run or execute against the isolated target simulator, then reconcile.

Approval in this build uses offline software keys. It is not phishing-resistant hardware authentication or a trusted display.
